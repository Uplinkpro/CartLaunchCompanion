# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project community.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Accepting feedback gracefully
- Focusing criticism on ideas and code, not people
- Helping newcomers participate
- Respecting privacy and intellectual property

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing private information, or intentionally disrupting project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate these standards. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile contact method. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from common open-source community standards.
